In March 2023 I presented my paper on the ASMO hardware platform at the [AUTOMED conference](https://www.thm.de/lse/en/forschung/ibmt/ag-biomedizinische-signale-und-systeme/automed-2023.html). ASMO is a decentralised platform that connects medical devices in intensive care and is built to stay verifiable, which matters because functional safety is critical there. The paper is listed under [publications](/publications/#asmo).
